## Exact Lean result

`MainTheorems.lean` proves three algebraic support results. The input identities are Appendix B equations (B9) and (B10), specialized to `dN = 0`. The first proof combines them with `linear_combination hB9 + (1 - s) * hB10`, yielding `dW = productivity + (1 - s) * relativePrice`. The next substitutes `relativePrice = -lambdaI / denominator`. The last proves the strict wage-rise condition by rewriting the equality and applying linear arithmetic.

These theorems use real-valued local changes as parameters. They do **not** derive (B9) or (B10) from the task continuum, market clearing, or the household problem. They do not construct an equilibrium, prove Proposition 3 in full, or certify any of the 19 named results. `PaperInterface.lean` and `ProofInterface.lean` therefore contain no claimed source-facing result row. The precise next proof obligation is the model-to-(B9)/(B10) bridge, including the relevant positive denominator, task threshold regime, and equilibrium comparative-static assumptions.
